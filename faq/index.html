Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about The Art of Mandala, the free mandala drawing app.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, with no sign-up and no ads.

Does it work on iPhone, iPad and Android?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. A tablet with a stylus is the most comfortable, but a finger or a mouse works fine too.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open mandalaforyou.com and start drawing. To keep it on your home screen, use “Add to Home Screen” (iPhone, iPad) or “Install app” (Android, Chrome).

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the app keeps working without internet, also when it is added to the home screen.

Can kids use it?

Yes. There is nothing to read or set up: kids just draw and the pattern appears. It is calm, has no ads and asks for no personal data.

How do I save my mandala?

Press Save: you get a 2K PNG image with your background and transparent corners. You can give the file a name before downloading.

Can I record a video of my drawing?

Yes. Press Rec before you start; when you stop, you get a time-lapse video of the whole drawing to download or share.

Can I sign my work?

Yes. In Canvas type your name: it is drawn along the bottom of the circle and is included in saved pictures and videos.

Is my drawing uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. Only anonymous visit statistics are counted, with no cookies.

Where can I see examples?

In the gallery on Pinterest and the Lotus lesson on YouTube. Step-by-step help is in How to draw a mandala.
