How to Draw a Mandala

Five simple steps from an empty circle to a finished mandala. You don’t need to draw well: the symmetry does most of the work.

1. Choose the number of segments

A mandala repeats the same shape around its centre. In The Art of Mandala open Symmetry and pick 6, 8 or 12 segments. Eight is a good start: the pattern is rich but every part is still easy to see. Turn on Axis if you want guide lines.

2. Start at the centre

Draw a small shape close to the middle: a dot, a short curve or a tiny petal. It appears in every segment at once, and the centre of your mandala is ready.

3. Grow it ring by ring

Move outwards one ring at a time. Each ring can repeat the idea of the one before it a little bigger: dots, then arcs, then petals. Leave a little empty space between rings so the eye can rest.

  • Use the round brush for dots and petals: press and drag.
  • Use the continuous line for arcs, waves and outlines.
  • Made a mistake? Undo takes back the last stroke.

4. Add color

Pick a brush theme (Lotus, Sea, Forest, Sari colors…): its shades appear next to the brush color, so the whole mandala stays in harmony. Switch shades from ring to ring. The metallic brush adds bronze, silver and brass accents; Glow makes the lines shine on a dark background.

5. Finish and save

Choose a background color or texture, sign your work along the edge of the circle, then Save a 2K image. Rec records a time-lapse video of the whole drawing, ready to share, and Live turns your mandala into a slowly spinning living wallpaper.
